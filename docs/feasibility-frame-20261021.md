# Feasibility preregistration note

Recorded on 21 October 2026 before selection research or dossier collection.

This note fixes preparation decisions for a small feasibility exercise. It is
not registration of the later model experiment. There will be no model calls,
paid APIs, quality scores or judgments about the selection institution.

## Frame and candidate pool

The edition is the 2026 Burgundy Grape selection. The listed candidate pool is
the complete official edition, including every published distinction. The
comparison pool is producing Burgundy estates in the official regional wine
body producer directory that do not appear in that complete edition. Search
engine absence alone never establishes that an estate is not listed.

Before proposing candidates, locate and verify both official sources. Preserve
the source URLs, retrieval times and membership lists privately, with a hash
of each snapshot. If the edition is incomplete, inaccessible or unavailable,
stop and request a source or amend this frame before proceeding. Do not invent
tiers or substitute a different edition, region or publisher.

### Geographic clarification recorded before candidate screening

The official edition covers the Côte de Nuits, Côte de Beaune and Côte
Chalonnaise. Restrict the comparison pool to producing estates in those same
areas. An estate outside the covered territory cannot fill the not listed
slot. This clarification follows verification of the official edition and
precedes candidate screening. The edition and selection rule are unchanged.

## Selection rule

1. Target four estates with distinct private IDs. Select one from the highest
   published Grape tier and one from the lowest published Grape tier.
2. Select one estate labeled Selected if that distinction exists in the
   verified edition. Otherwise use the middle published Grape tier. If neither
   exists, use a second distinct estate from the lowest tier and disclose this.
3. Select one verified not listed estate with sparse accessible documentation.
   If sparse documentation and not listed membership cannot be combined, use
   one not listed estate plus a fifth estate meeting the sparse condition.
4. Within each slot, order eligible candidates by normalized official name and
   propose the first candidate that meets the slot requirements. Keep the
   candidate pool and names in private records only. No estate is selected on
   the basis of a model response or a quality judgment.
5. Selection screening is separate from dossier collection. For sparse status,
   use up to five minutes per candidate, at most five candidates, with fixed
   query categories: official producer documentation, certification or registry
   documentation, and independent documentary reporting. Exclude tasting notes.
   Record the queries, access limits and time without extracting dossier facts.
6. Sparse means no more than one accessible independent documentary source was
   found within this screen. This is a bounded search result, not proof that
   evidence does not exist. Retain the sparse estate after approval even if
   collection later remains difficult. If the screen cannot establish an
   eligible sparse candidate, stop rather than quietly relaxing the rule.
7. Submit the private shortlist and reasons for approval before collecting
   evidence. Personal or trade objections may veto a candidate. Log the reason
   privately and apply the same slot rule to its replacement before collection.
   Publish only the existence of an amendment, never private reasons or names.

## Collection boundaries after approval

Use public documentary facts and links with one source per observation.
Record rights as unknown when unknown. Never copy paid tasting notes, bypass
access restrictions or infer sensory evidence from a website or a critic.
All tasting criteria remain absent unless observations are supplied directly
by the study lead. Private expected labels remain null until adjudicated by
the study lead and a second reviewer before any future model run.

## Measures and stopping points

Record time for searching, reading, structuring, rights review and leak review.
Record every search including unsuccessful searches. Track source access and
rights status, aliases and manual or automatic leaks, and completeness for
every rubric criterion. Selected and not listed remain distinct statuses.

Actual names, source text that identifies an estate, private review notes and
approval records stay in ignored storage with owner only permissions. Public
output contains aliases and aggregate measures only. It will not rank or
disparage a named producer. Public prose will contain no dash punctuation,
employer, city, age or claims of superior model objectivity.

Stop at the private shortlist for approval. After approved collection, stop
again with feasibility measures and review sheets for adjudication. Do not
build the public report, publish real summaries, touch the website, deploy,
draft an essay or register the model experiment before the required approvals.
